Mathematical Formulas & Calculation Rules

Gold melting calculation in traditional and modern jewellery manufacturing relies on the law of conservation of mass and fine gold purity ratios. Here is the exact mathematical model implemented in My Gold Melting:

1. Total Fine Gold Content

When mixing multiple batches of scrap gold, ornaments, or bullion bars, each batch has a weight $w_i$ and touch $t_i$. The total fine gold contained in the melt is:

Pure Fine Gold Formula
Total Fine Gold = Σ (Weight_i × Touch_i) / 100

2. Required Target Weight

To convert the entire melt into the desired output touch $T_{\text{target}}$, the required final weight of the alloy is:

Theoretical Target Weight Formula
Target Weight = (Total Fine Gold / Target Touch) × 100

3. Addition Decision: Add Palo vs. Add Fine Gold

  • When Total Batch Weight < Target Weight: The current metal is purer than required. You must add base alloy metal (Palo) such as copper or silver:
    Add Palo Formula
    Add Palo = Target Weight - Total Batch Weight Final Weight = Total Batch Weight + Add Palo
  • When Total Batch Weight > Target Weight: The current metal purity is lower than required. You must add pure 100% Fine Gold:
    Add Fine Gold Formula
    Add Fine Gold = ((Total Batch Weight - Target Weight) × Target Touch) / (100 - Target Touch) Final Weight = Total Batch Weight + Add Fine Gold

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Touch" mean in gold melting?
In gold smithing and hallmarking, "Touch" (or Touche / टंच) denotes the percentage purity of precious metal out of 100. For example, 22K gold has a touch of 91.6%, while 18K gold has a touch of 75.0%.
What is Palo in Indian gold melting?
Palo (पालो) is the base alloy metal (commonly oxygen-free copper, fine silver, zinc, or brass) melted together with pure gold to lower its touch down to an exact ornament specification such as 91.6% or 75.0%.
Can I calculate silver melting with this tool?
Yes. Silver purity follows the identical mathematical touch and ratio formulas (e.g. 92.5% Sterling Silver, 80.0% touch silver, or 99.9% fine silver). Simply enter the weights and silver touches as normal.
Is this calculator completely private and offline?
Yes. All calculations are executed strictly inside your device's browser memory using client-side JavaScript. No numbers, weights, or business secrets are ever transmitted to remote servers.
