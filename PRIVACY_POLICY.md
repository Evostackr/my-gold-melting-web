## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **My Gold Melting**, a specialized utility created for goldsmiths, jewellers, and craftsmen to determine gold and silver melting purity, touches, and jewellery costing. This application is designed and maintained by **Evostackr**.

We value your privacy and believe in complete transparency. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the My Gold Melting web application.

---

## 2. Offline-First Architecture & Zero Data Collection
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, harvest, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information, contact books, biometric data, or financial credentials.
* **No Server Storage:** All calculations—including gold weights, touche percentages, output touches, palo additions, fine gold calculations, and jewellery casting expenses—are processed strictly on your local device.
* **Zero Remote Telemetry:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & History Retention
* **Local Persistence:** To provide a convenient experience, your calculation history is saved locally on your device using client-side IndexedDB and LocalStorage in your browser.
* **User Control:** You retain 100% control over your data. You may delete individual calculation records or erase your entire calculation history at any time from within the application.
* **Data Portability:** Your stored data never leaves your device without your explicit action (such as manually using the system Share button).

---

## 4. Permissions & Device Capabilities
* My Gold Melting requires **no sensitive device permissions** (such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, or SMS).
* Web runtime network connectivity is used solely to load static web fonts (Google Fonts) and app assets.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
* We do not sell or share data with advertising networks or data brokers.
* Any external links (such as developer website or support email) are opened directly in your default browser or mail client under their respective terms.

---

## 6. Security
Because all calculations and records are processed and stored locally on your device, your information is protected by your device’s security mechanisms and browser sandboxing.
