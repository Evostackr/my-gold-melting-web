About

The greatest application for goldsmiths to manage melting and determine the purity of gold through applications is this one.

  • Multiple calculations made simultaneously
  • Simple-to-use calculator
  • Enter the total gram and melting value to determine your total melting.

  • Multiple melting support
  • Costing simplified

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: Immediately

Welcome to My Gold Melting. This application is designed, developed, and maintained by Evostackr (Developer: Ujwal Anand). We are dedicated to providing a secure, reliable, and privacy-respecting utility for goldsmiths, jewellers, and craftsmen.

1. Offline-First & Zero Data Collection

My Gold Melting operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, harvest, track, sell, or transmit any personal information, contact lists, financial details, or device identifiers.

2. Local Storage & Calculations Privacy

All calculations—including gold weights, touche purity, output touche, palo additions, fine gold amounts, and jewellery casting expenses—are processed and retained exclusively on your device using local browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage). Your calculations never leave your device.

3. Permissions & Device Access

This application does not request access to sensitive device permissions like your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or file storage. It requires only standard browser runtime access.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data-mining brokers. Typography assets are loaded via Google Fonts with privacy protection.

5. Data Ownership and Deletion

You own all your calculation and history records. You can delete individual records or clear your entire calculation history at any time from within the History screen.

6. Security

Because all data remains local to your device or browser session, your calculations are protected by your device's security and browser sandboxing.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly within the application and on our official website.
