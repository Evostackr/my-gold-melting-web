About Evostackr

Engineering High-Precision Software for Goldsmiths

Empowering traditional goldsmiths, jewelers, assayers, and bullion craftsmen with modern, privacy-first, offline computation suites.

Our Mission & Core Philosophy

Gold and silver smithing is an ancient craft combining intricate art with exact metallurgy. For decades, goldsmiths performed complex multi-sample purity averaging and Palo addition formulas manually on paper or rudimentary pocket calculators—wasting valuable time and risking costly rounding errors.

My Gold Melting was built to modernize this process while respecting the practical realities of the workshop:

  • Zero Internet Dependency: Workshop basements, secure vaults, and refinery floors frequently have poor network reception. Our suite operates 100% offline.
  • Absolute Data Privacy: Bullion weights, precious metal rates, and customer transactions are strictly confidential. We enforce a zero-telemetry policy—all records remain on your local device.
  • Precision Mathematical Models: All equations use 3-decimal floating-point arithmetic (0.000g) tested against certified hallmarking laboratories.
