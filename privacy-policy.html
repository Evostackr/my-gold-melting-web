Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: Immediately

Welcome to My Gold Melting. This application is designed, developed, and maintained by Evostackr (Developer: Ujwal Anand). We are dedicated to providing a secure, reliable, and privacy-respecting utility for goldsmiths, jewellers, and craftsmen.

1. Offline-First & Zero Remote Data Collection

My Gold Melting operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, harvest, track, sell, or transmit any personal information, customer contacts, financial figures, or device identifiers to any remote server or third party.

2. Local Storage & Calculations Privacy

All calculations—including gold weights, touche purity, output touche, palo additions, fine gold amounts, and jewellery casting expenses—are processed and retained exclusively on your device using client-side browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage). Your calculations never leave your device.

3. Permissions & Device Access

This application does not request access to sensitive device permissions like your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or local file system. It requires only standard browser runtime access.

4. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral cookies, or data-mining brokers. Typography assets are loaded via Google Fonts with privacy protection.

5. Data Ownership and Deletion

You own all your calculation and history records. You can delete individual records or clear your entire calculation history at any time from within the History screen inside the app.

6. Security

Because all data remains local to your device or browser session, your calculations are protected by your device's security and browser sandboxing.
